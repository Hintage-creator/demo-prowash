Material concept

The pattern lives inside the colour.

Water is the key. While the fabric is dry, the print stays hidden in the burgundy. As rain soaks in, the cheetah comes forward. The effect is tonal by design: no bright contrast, only depth.

  1. 01

    Dry

    Solid burgundy. The print is there, but the eye can't find it.

  2. 02

  3. 03

Details

Made to be looked at twice.

Four things define the jacket. Three of them you see right away.

Dry tone Burgundy
Wet tone Cheetah
Colour

Deep burgundy

A dense red that leans toward black in low light and stays rich in daylight. The cheetah sits in the same family, a few shades darker.

Silhouette

Hooded and full

A generous, rounded volume finished with a hood. Substantial in shape, clean in line.

Texture

Horizontal channels

Even stitch lines divide the fill into horizontal channels. Every ridge catches light, and every seam holds shadow.

Design

The tonal cheetah

Dark on burgundy, never loud. It shows up when the weather does and keeps the jacket quiet the rest of the time.
